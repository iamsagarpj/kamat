## 5. Positioning finding (important)

The brief frames him as a business leader first. His public profiles show a **dual identity**: an elected Zilla Parishad member whose personal brand ("Voice of Khed", "For the People · With the People") is built on public service, and a construction entrepreneur since 2023.

The site keeps the brief's "individual, not just Vijaya Construction" principle and presents both sides together under one line:

> **"Building Khed. Serving Satara."** (alternatives: "Development, delivered." / "From Khed, for the people.")

**Please confirm.** Because he is both an elected representative and the owner of a *government contracting* firm, the client (and ideally his advisor) should decide how prominently the two are shown side by side on one site. The architecture supports either emphasis with a content change only.
